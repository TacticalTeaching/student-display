Helpful hints

  • Ones under ones, tens under tens. The bigger number goes on top.
  • Subtract the ones first, then move left one column at a time.
  • If the top digit is smaller, exchange 1 from the next column. 1 ten = 10 ones. 52 − 18: 5 tens → 4, 2 ones → 12
  • If the next column is 0, keep going left until you find a digit to exchange. 500 − 128: 5 → 4, 0 → 9, 0 → 10
  • Line up the decimal points. The point drops straight down into the answer. 46.3 − 18.75 → 46.30 − 18.75
  • Adding a zero to the end of a decimal does not change its value. You may need to exchange into it. 46.3 = 46.30
  • Add your answer to the bottom number. You should get the top number. 605 − 247 = 358 → 358 + 247 = 605
