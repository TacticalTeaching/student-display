Helpful hints

  • Multiply the ones digit of the top number first, then move left.
  • Always multiply first, then add the carried number. 4 × 6 + 2 = 26
  • In row 2 you multiply by tens, so put a 0 in the ones first. 347 × 20 = 6940
  • Cross out the row 1 carries before you start row 2 so you do not add them again.
  • Add row 1 and row 2 using column addition to get the answer. 1388 + 6940 = 8328
  • Ignore the point and multiply. Then count the decimal places in the question and put the same number in the answer. 4.56 × 7 → 456 × 7 = 3192 → 31.92
  • Round first to check your answer makes sense. 347 × 24 ≈ 350 × 20 = 7000
