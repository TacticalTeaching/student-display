## 2. Quick Reference for the 4 Existing Prototypes

### A. Expanding Numbers (`expanding-numbers.html` — Family 1, `pv`)
- **Year 1 (`B1`):** 2-digit numbers (`64 = 6 tens + 4 ones = 60 + 4`)
- **Year 2 (`C1`, `C4`):** Up to 120 including zero tens (`107 = 1 hundred + 0 tens + 7 ones = 100 + 7`)
- **Year 3 (`D1`, `D3`):** 3-digit numbers (`325 = 300 + 20 + 5`)
- **Year 4 (`E1`):** 4-digit numbers (`4,382 = 4,000 + 300 + 80 + 2`)
- **Year 5 (`F1`):** 5- and 6-digit numbers (`348,502 = 300,000 + 40,000 + 8,000 + 500 + 2`)
- **Year 6 (`G1`):** 7+ digit numbers (millions)
- **Year 7 (`H1`, `H2`):** Expanded form with **powers of 10** (`34,506 = 3×10⁴ + 4×10³ + 5×10² + 6`)
- **Year 8 (`I1`, `I2`, `I3`):** Decimals with **positive & negative powers of 10** (`3.61 = 3 + 6×10⁻¹ + 1×10⁻²`)

### B. Multiplication & Division Step-by-Step (`multiplication-division.html` — Family 7, `md`)
- **Year 1 (`B17`):** Equal groups up to 20 (`4 groups of 3 = 12`)
- **Year 2 (`C18`–`C20`):** `2, 5, 10` tables array & skip counting (`6 × 5 = 30`)
- **Year 3 (`D13`–`D16`):** `2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 10` tables + **2-digit × 1-digit partitioning** (`23 × 2 = 20×2 + 3×2 = 46`)
- **Year 4 (`E7`–`E10`):** **2- and 3-digit × 1-digit** (`243 × 3 = 729`) + **exact division by 1-digit** (`65 ÷ 5 = 13`)
- **Year 5 (`F9`–`F12`):** **3/4-digit × 1-digit** (`1,234 × 6`), **2-digit × 2-digit** (`34 × 27`), and **division with remainder** (`278 ÷ 4 = 69 r 2`)
- **Year 6 (`G9`–`G12`):** **3/4-digit × 2-digit** (`542 × 12`), **5-digit ÷ 1-digit** (`1,283 ÷ 5`), and **remainder converter** (`31 ÷ 6 = 5 r 1 = 5⅙ ≈ 5.17`)
- **Year 7 (`H14`, `H15`, `H24`):** **3-digit × 2-digit** (`327 × 24`), **divide by 2-digit** (`768 ÷ 24 = 32`, `327 ÷ 12 = 27¼ = 27.25`), and **decimal × whole** (`0.7 × 5 = 3.5`)
- **Year 8 (`I11`, `I18`):** Divide to decimal/fraction (`327 ÷ 15 = 21.8 = 21⅘`) + **multiply decimals** (`2.3 × 45 = 103.5`)

### C. Order of Operations Calculator (`order-of-operations.html` — Family 8, `oo`)
- **Year 6 (`G13`):** Whole numbers with Brackets `( )` and `+ − × ÷` (`3 + 4 × 2 = 11`)
- **Year 7 (`H16`):** Adds **Exponents (`²`, `³`)** and roots (`(3 + 5) × 2² = 32`)
- **Year 8 (`I12`):** Adds **Negative Integers** (`−3 + 4 × −2 = −11`)

### D. Protractor & Angles (`protractor-angles.html` — Family 15, `ang` + `sr`)
- **Year 2–3 (`C35`, `D35`):** Full, `½`, `¼`, `¾` turns
- **Year 4 (`E36`):** Estimate & compare to `90°`, `180°`, `360°` benchmarks
- **Year 5 (`F42`, `F43`):** Classify (`acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex`) + **180° protractor reading & drawing** (`65°`, inner vs outer scale)
- **Year 6 (`G40`–`G42`, `G48`):** **360° reflex protractor** (`210°`) + missing angles on a straight line (`180°`), at a point (`360°`), vertically opposite
- **Year 7 (`H50`, `H51`):** Parallel lines (`F, Z, C` angles) + algebraic angle equations (`2x + 30° = 90°`)
- **Year 8 (`I48`, `I49`):** Polygon interior (`(n − 2) × 180°`) and exterior (`360° ÷ n`) angle reasoning
