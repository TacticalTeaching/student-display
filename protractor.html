Helpful hints

  • Put the centre dot on the corner of the angle, and the base line along one arm.
  • Use the scale that starts at 0 on the arm lying along the base line.
  • Is it smaller or bigger than a square corner? That tells you which number makes sense. Smaller than 90° → 50°, not 130°
  • Acute: less than 90°. Right: exactly 90°. Obtuse: between 90° and 180°.
  • Straight: exactly 180°. Reflex: more than 180°. Reflex = 360° − angle
  • Quarter turn = 90°. Half turn = 180°. Three-quarter turn = 270°. Full turn = 360°.
  • Read both arms on the same scale, then subtract the smaller from the bigger. 142° − 37° = 105°
