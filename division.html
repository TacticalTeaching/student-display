Helpful hints

  • Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down. Repeat until there are no digits left. Does McDonald's Sell Burgers?
  • If the divisor does not go into the first digit, use the first two digits. 258 ÷ 4: 4 does not go into 2, so use 25
  • If the number is too small, write 0 above and bring down the next digit. 3 ÷ 4 = 0, bring down
  • Write the first few multiples of the divisor to help you estimate. 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90
  • What is left at the end is the remainder. It must be smaller than the divisor. 258 ÷ 4 = 64 r 2
  • Put a decimal point after the dividend and in the answer, then add zeros and keep dividing. 327 ÷ 15 = 21.8
  • Answer × divisor + remainder should equal the dividend. 64 × 4 + 2 = 258
