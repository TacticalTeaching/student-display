Helpful hints

  • Ones under ones, tens under tens, hundreds under hundreds.
  • Add the ones first, then move left one column at a time.
  • If a column makes 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry the ten to the next column. 8 + 7 = 15 → write 5, carry 1
  • Remember to add the carried number into the next column. 1 + 5 + 4 = 10
  • Line up the decimal points. The point drops straight down into the answer. 34.6 + 7.25 → 34.60 + 7.25
  • Adding a zero to the end of a decimal does not change its value. 34.6 = 34.60
  • Round each number first to check your answer makes sense. 358 + 247 ≈ 360 + 250 = 610
